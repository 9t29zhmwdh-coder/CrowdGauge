# Code of Conduct

## Pledge

I as the maintainer pledge to make participation in this project a harassment free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the project

Behaviour that does not:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind
- Trolling, insulting comments and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing other people's private information without explicit permission

## Enforcement

I am responsible for clarifying these standards and will take fair corrective action in response to
behaviour I consider inappropriate. I may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

Report unacceptable behaviour through my GitHub profile. I will review every report and respond in a
way that I judge appropriate to the circumstances, keeping the reporter's identity confidential.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
